Grade 7+ Interactive Math Toolkit

Designed for middle and high school algebra, coordinate geometry, trigonometry, and teacher quizzes!

🔒 Teacher / Quiz Mode ("Guess to Reveal")

When Teachers enable Quiz Mode in Settings, all canvas measurements (distances $d$, hypotenuse $c$, angles, circle areas, polygon perimeters) are hidden behind a "?" badge. Students can click or tap any "?" to open an interactive check modal, calculate the answer on paper or using the built-in calculator, and submit their guess to unlock a green "✓ Verified" badge!

📐 Geometry Tools
  • Polygon [G]: Tap vertices and double-click or tap start point to close. Calculates Shoelace Area & Perimeter!
  • Transform [M]: Reflect points or line segments across X-axis, Y-axis, or $y=x$.
  • Right Δ [R]: Pythagorean theorem ($a^2 + b^2 = c^2$).
  • Angle ∠ABC [A]: Measures degrees, radians, and classifies acute, right 90°, or obtuse angles.
🧮 Scientific Calculator & Solvers

Access the Calculator tab in the sidebar to evaluate expressions, convert fractions ($a/b$), calculate roots, or solve quadratic equations ($ax^2 + bx + c = 0$) instantly.
